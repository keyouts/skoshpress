PRIVACY POLICY

Skosh Press · KeyOuts
Effective October 11, 2026.

DATA COLLECTION

Skosh Press does not require an account or collect usage data. It has no ads, analytics or automatic crash reporting.

LOCAL FILES

Projects, imports and exports are handled on your computer. The app does not upload your work. You choose where to save files. Preferences, templates, printer profiles, dictionaries and recovery data may be kept in local application storage.

INTERNET & SHARING

Publishing tools work offline. There is no cloud sync or automatic updater. If you share an exported file through another service, that service's privacy terms apply.

REMOVING DATA

Delete saved files where you stored them. Local settings or recovery files may remain after uninstalling; remove the application's data folder to clear them.

POLICY CHANGES

Changes to this policy will be included with a software release.

    ADVANCED SPACING & KERNING

    Set ink sidebearings non-destructively. Right bearing adjusts the advance width; left bearing shifts the exported outline. Proof in the word workspace.

    Use reusable left/right glyph classes for optical kerning. An explicit pair (above) overrides its class value. Exports include GPOS pair positioning and a compatible legacy kern table.

    SHAPE & PRECISION TOOLS

    Transforms edit the actual Bézier geometry, so changes appear in your exported font. Every action supports glyph undo.

    OUTLINE BUILDER · WEIGHT & BOOLEAN SHAPES

    Build weight directly into the editable letter outlines, or combine two contours. Effects are applied permanently to the chosen glyph and can be undone with Undo glyph. Save font to include them in the exported TTF.

    Small adjustments work best (try +10 to +30). Positive values grow outside edges and shrink counters (holes). Negative values thin the glyph. Complex/self-overlapping contours may need manual cleanup.

    Use the Contour A/B selectors to choose two shapes. Combine merges them; Subtract cuts B out of A; Intersect keeps only their overlap. Other contours stay unchanged. Boolean results remain editable Bézier outlines.
